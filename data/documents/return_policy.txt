Northstar Office Supply - Return Policy (fictional test document)
Unopened products in original packaging may be returned within 30 calendar days after delivery with proof of purchase.
Opened software licenses are not refundable. Defective hardware can be exchanged within 14 days after delivery.
Refunds for eligible returns are issued to the original payment method within 7 business days after inspection.
Customers must request a return authorization before shipping an item back.
